About Zachary Dorf

CEO & Co-Founder of Bask Health

Zachary Dorf is a New York City-based entrepreneur, engineer, and data scientist — and the CEO & Co-Founder of Bask Health, the leading telehealth infrastructure platform often described as "the Shopify for telehealth."

At his core, Zachary is an engineer and data scientist who builds from first principles. He doesn't start with what exists — he starts with what should exist, and works backward from there. It's the same instinct that led him from throwing streetwear events as a teenager to architecting healthcare infrastructure that now serves millions of patients. For Zachary, talent is overrated. What matters is relentless effort, disciplined execution, and the willingness to keep building when everyone else has moved on. Ideas are cheap. Shipping is everything.

Background

Born and raised in New York City, Zachary has been an entrepreneur his entire life. His journey began in streetwear and fashion, founding Solepreme Con and organizing dozens of events across New York City, Los Angeles, and London. The events attracted significant attention, earning coverage from Hypebeast, Complex, and the LA Times.

Zachary went on to attend the Leeds School of Business at the University of Colorado Boulder for his undergraduate degree. After graduating in May 2020, he launched a Covid supply company before turning his attention to healthcare technology.

The Bask Health Story

Together with his twin brother Elias Dorf, Zachary founded what would become Bask Health. The brothers initially spent nine months and practically every dollar they had trying to sell eyelash enhancement products through a direct-to-consumer telehealth platform called Lash by Bask. Google and Facebook wouldn't let them advertise prescription products. Customer acquisition was impossible. They ran out of money.

Both were ready to go back to their old careers — Zach to data science, Eli to healthcare venture capital. Then the emails started coming in. Entrepreneurs had found their dormant site and wanted to know: can you help us build a telehealth platform like this? Not one or two. Dozens.

The brothers realized they'd been solving the wrong problem. The opportunity wasn't selling through telehealth. It was building the infrastructure that made telehealth businesses possible.

As one engineer and one life sciences professional, they became frustrated by the lack of technology and infrastructure available to help non-healthcare entrepreneurs launch in the space. They took matters into their own hands to build Bask Health — a Shopify-style solution for telehealth.

Bask Health

Bask Health provides a full-service software (SaaS) platform that allows anyone to build any digital health experience. Designed for doctors, physicians, entrepreneurs, and developers, the Bask system is built at an enterprise scale for the everyday user. The platform enables everyone to create and grow a direct-to-consumer telehealth business.

From online consultations to e-prescribing and pharmacy fulfillment, Bask handles the entire telehealth commerce experience. The platform offers customizable white-label solutions, drag-and-drop questionnaire builders, integrated patient, doctor and admin portals, secure analytics, and nationwide pharmacy fulfillment delivering medications to all 50 states.
